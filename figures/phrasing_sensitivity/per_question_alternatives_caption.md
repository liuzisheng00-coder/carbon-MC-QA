### Alternative A — paired delta plot

**Figure X | Change in per-question performance under alternative phrasings.** Points show the change in correct-run count for P1 and P2 relative to the benchmark wording P0. Each count is based on three repetitions. Negative values indicate fewer correct runs than P0; zero indicates no change. The figure preserves the paired question structure and directly identifies which alternative wording produced the performance loss.

### Alternative B — operation-faceted trajectories

**Figure X | Per-question response profiles across alternative wordings.** Lines connect the number of correct outputs across three repetitions for P0, P1 and P2. Questions with identical trajectories are combined and directly labelled. Black lines identify stable profiles, whereas blue lines identify profiles that changed across wordings. Faceting by operation prevents overlapping trajectories from obscuring operation-specific patterns.

### Alternative C — sensitivity-gap lollipop plot

**Figure X | Magnitude of per-question phrasing sensitivity.** Sensitivity is defined as the range between the maximum and minimum correct-run counts across P0, P1 and P2. A gap of zero indicates an invariant profile, whereas a gap of three indicates the maximum observed sensitivity. This summary ranks questions by sensitivity magnitude but intentionally does not identify which wording caused the loss.

Statistics note: all three alternatives present paired descriptive counts from three repetitions per wording. No inferential test or confidence interval is applied because repetitions are not treated as independent question samples.
